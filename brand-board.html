Visual Guidelines

Photography: Warm, moody, golden-hour tones. Dark wooden surfaces, linen cloth, candlelight, pine branches for Christmas. Rich, saturated cake close-ups. Always warm colour grading, never cool or clinical.

Packaging: Kraft/cream base with botanical plum branch illustrations in deep plum and forest green. Gold foil accents for premium lines. Gift tags tied with plum-coloured ribbon.

Illustrations: Hand-drawn botanical style — plum branches with leaves and fruit. Victorian scrollwork and ornamental frames. Always in the four-colour palette.

Video / Reels: Close-ups of mixing, pouring, slicing. Steam, texture, the reveal of dried fruit inside. Overhead shots on wooden boards. Warm, nostalgic colour grading. Text overlays in Playfair Display.

Logo clear space: One plum-width of empty space around the logo on all sides.
