Usage

Ruby is the brand primary — headlines, the crest outline, key accents. It grabs attention on any feed.

Honey Gold is the luxury signal — crown, cake stand, labels, decorative lines. Use it for premium emphasis.

Forest is for leaf details and freshness cues only. Never as a primary.

Ivory / Champagne are the light backgrounds. Espresso is body text.

WhatsApp green (#25D366) appears only on the order button.

Usage Guidelines

Logo clear space: Keep at least the width of the crown above all sides of the crest.

Minimum size: Full crest needs 160px width minimum. Icon mark works down to 32px.

Never: Stretch, rotate, recolour with non-brand colours, or place on a busy photo without a solid backing.

Photography: Warm golden-hour light, rich saturated tones. Show the cake's texture and the deep plum colour. Wooden boards, linen cloth, warm backdrops. No clinical white, no cool blue tones.

Video / Reels: Close-up of cutting the cake. Steam rising. Glaze dripping. The reveal of the plum interior. Overhead pours. Hands-in-frame. Always warm colour grading.
