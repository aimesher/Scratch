Usage

Plum Deep is the primary brand colour, used for headlines, the logo mark, and key accents. Burnished Gold is the secondary accent for labels, dividers, and calls to action. Walnut is the body text colour. Cream and Rose Mist are background and surface colours. WhatsApp green (#25D366) is used only for the order button.

Usage Notes

Logo clear space: Keep at least the width of one plum from the mark around all sides.

Minimum size: The icon mark works down to 24px. The full logo needs at least 120px width.

Backgrounds: Use the light logo on dark surfaces. Use the dark logo on cream and white. Never place on busy photographs without a solid or semi-transparent backing.

Photography style: Warm natural light, shallow depth of field, textured surfaces (linen, wood, parchment). Show the cake's texture and the plums. Avoid harsh flash and clinical white backgrounds.
