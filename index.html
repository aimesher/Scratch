Our Story

The Old Plum House was born from a family recipe passed down through generations. We bake the way it was always done — by hand, in small batches, with no shortcuts and no compromises.

Every cake is made with real butter, farm-fresh eggs, and plums selected at the peak of their season. No preservatives. No artificial flavours. Just honest baking.
